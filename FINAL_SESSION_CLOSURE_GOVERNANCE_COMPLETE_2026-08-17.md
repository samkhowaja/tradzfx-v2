## 🎓 LESSONS LEARNED: GOVERNANCE AT SCALE

1. **Fail-closed semantics must be proven end-to-end** (not just one layer)
   - Database, application, and backtest each need enforcement
   - Chain is only as strong as weakest link

2. **Multiple competing semantics require explicit source-of-truth declaration**
   - v1, v2, v3, v4 detectors can coexist if canonical is clear
   - Ambiguity creates risk; clarity creates confidence

3. **Typed contracts (booleans, enums) more robust than inferred semantics**
   - "permission = INACTIVE/CONDITIONAL/ACTIVE" > "is_frozen = true/false"
   - State machines more auditable than boolean flags

4. **Governance freezes require multi-layer enforcement**
   - Technical (database READ-ONLY views, app gates)
   - Organizational (board decisions, signature blocks)
   - Operational (escalation matrix, contingency procedures)

5. **Documentation-first approach enables rapid context recovery**
   - Compaction happens; detailed traces survive
   - Runbooks are executable; specifications are referential

6. **Board sessions need ready-to-execute workflows, not aspirational plans**
   - 90-min timeline with printed materials beats "work with leadership"
   - Go/no-go gates at each step beats open-ended reviews
